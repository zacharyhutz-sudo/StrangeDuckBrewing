BEFORE YOU FLY OVER

A few good
things to know.

Can I bring the family?+

Absolutely. Strange Duck is a family-friendly brewery with mini golf and outdoor space to enjoy together. Alcohol is available only to guests 21 and older.

Can I bring my dog?+

Dogs are welcome in the patio and picnic areas. Keep your pup leashed and check with the team for any current restrictions.

What about food?+

Food trucks are part of the brewery’s event mix. Check Facebook for scheduled vendors or call before you come; food availability varies.

Do I need to book mini golf?+

Call the taproom for current mini-golf pricing, availability, and arrangements for larger groups.

Where can I find upcoming music and events?+

The brewery’s Facebook page is the best place to check upcoming shows, special events, and the latest updates.

Can I plan a group visit or event?+

Get in touch with the team by phone or Facebook to discuss your date, group size, and plans. The team can confirm what’s possible.
