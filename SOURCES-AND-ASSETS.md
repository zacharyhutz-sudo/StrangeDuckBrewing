## Generated art brief

Built-in image generation produced a dark-teal, screen-printed, 1970s-inspired folk floral background with mint leaves, warm cream, yellow, coral, and magenta flowers. The composition leaves a broad dark center-left area for web copy. No text or logo was generated. Final deliverable path: `assets/images/floral.webp`.
